Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

OwnIt helps you use social media on purpose. This page explains what we collect and why. In short: we keep only what the app needs to work, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and name. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
  • Your reality check answers: the apps you use, roughly how long you use them, and the goals you set.
  • Your visits: what you said you were looking for, which app you went to, the time you planned, when you started and finished, and your check-in answer.
  • Videos you save: only the ones you choose to share to OwnIt: the link, its title and picture, and the category you put it in.
  • Notification details: if you turn on notifications, a technical address your browser gives us so we can send you "time's up" alerts.

What we don't collect

OwnIt does not see what you watch, like, post or message on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or any other app. It only knows about a video when you share it to OwnIt yourself. We don't track you across other websites, and we don't show ads.

How we use it

Only to run OwnIt for you: to sign you in, show your daily budget and weekly insights, and send the notifications you asked for.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored securely with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the European Union. Each account can only read its own data. The website is hosted by Netlify.

Sharing

We don't sell or share your data with anyone, except the service providers above that run OwnIt for us, or if the law requires it.

Your choices

You can change your answers and goals at any time in your Profile, and turn notifications off in your browser or phone settings. To delete your account and all of its data, contact us through the support email shown on OwnIt's Google sign-in screen, and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
